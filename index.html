I am a Ph.D. candidate in Information Systems at the W. P. Carey School of Business, Arizona State University, advised by Pei-yu (Sharon) Chen and Zhan (Michael) Shi.

My research studies how algorithms and AI agents reshape markets: how firms set prices algorithmically, how AI shopping agents change the way consumers search and buy, and how interdependent technologies such as pricing and recommendation systems interact on digital platforms. I combine analytical modeling, econometrics, and field experiments, drawing on machine learning and deep learning where the data call for it.
